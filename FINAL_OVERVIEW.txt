HOW THE 20-MIN SESSION NOW WORKS
================================================================================

OPENING (1-2 min):
Create safety. "What do you actually feel?"

DEEP INQUIRY (10-12 min):
Ask Socratic questions. "What does that protect you from?"
"Is that true about you - or a belief?"

INSIGHT (4-5 min):
Help them see their conditioning. Without judgment.

INTEGRATION (2-3 min):
"This week, just notice. No fixing yet."

================================================================================
MAGIC QUESTIONS (Core Tools)
================================================================================

What does this pattern protect you from?
Is that actually true - or a belief from someone else?
What would happen if you didn't need this protection?
Where did you learn that? Who taught you that about yourself?
Can you feel what that fear actually is? (Not think - feel)
If you saw this clearly with no story, what would you see?
Can you have compassion for the scared part of you?
Who would you be if you didn't have that story?
