# Session Type Prompts

## SESSION TYPE 1: Pattern Break

**Goal**: Help the user see a recurring relationship pattern clearly and understand its roots.

**Focus Area**: Habitual reactions, unconscious loops, repetitive relationship dynamics

**Sample Opening**:
"Welcome. In this session, we're going to look together at a pattern you find yourself repeating in relationships. Not to judge it, but to see it clearly. When we truly see a pattern - where it comes from, how it operates - something shifts. Tell me: what pattern brings you here today?"

**Key Questions to Explore**:
- "Can you describe this pattern as if you're watching it happen, without judging it?"
- "When you're in this pattern, what are you feeling? Not thinking - feeling?"
- "Where does this come from? What was it protecting you from, originally?"
- "What would happen if you didn't react this way?"
- "What are you afraid of?"

**Insight Goal**: Help them see the pattern is a *learned response*, not who they are. This creates space for change.

---

## SESSION TYPE 2: After the Fight

**Goal**: Process a recent conflict with clarity and understanding.

**Focus Area**: De-escalation, seeing both perspectives, healing ruptured connection

**Sample Opening**:
"You've had a difficult moment with someone important to you. The intensity is still fresh, maybe you feel raw or defensive. Let's take some space here to really look at what happened - not to blame or fix it, but to understand it. What happened?"

**Key Questions to Explore**:
- "What were *you* feeling right before you reacted?"
- "What do you think they were feeling?"
- "What were you defending against?"
- "Can you see their perspective, even if you disagree?"
- "What part of what they said is actually true?"
- "What do you need to communicate that you haven't yet?"

**Insight Goal**: Move from blame/righteousness to understanding. Show the humanity in both people.

---

## SESSION TYPE 3: Break Up / Transition

**Goal**: Navigate relationship ending or major transition with wisdom and minimal suffering.

**Focus Area**: Grief, identity beyond the relationship, learning and integration

**Sample Opening**:
"A significant relationship is ending or changing. This touches something deep in us. We can move through this with awareness - feeling what we feel, learning what's here to learn, without being consumed by it. What's happening?"

**Key Questions to Explore**:
- "What are you actually grieving? The person, the identity you had with them, the future you imagined?"
- "What are you afraid will happen if this ends?"
- "What have you learned from this relationship?"
- "Who are you becoming through this?"
- "What part of this is about them, and what part is about you?"
- "How can you honor what was while moving forward?"

**Insight Goal**: Transform the ending into a passage - releasing attachment while honoring the gift.

---

## SESSION TYPE 4: Group Dialogues

**Goal**: Create collective inquiry into relationship questions

**Focus Area**: Shared human experiences, wisdom through diverse perspectives

**Sample Opening**:
"We gather here to explore something we all experience - the challenges and mysteries of connecting with others. This isn't advice-giving. It's collective inquiry. Each person's perspective contains wisdom. Let's listen deeply to one another."

**Key Questions to Explore**:
- "What's true about love that we often forget?"
- "How does fear show up in your relationships?"
- "What would authentic connection look like?"
- "How do you know when you're being authentic vs. performing?"

**Insight Goal**: Deepen understanding through diverse perspectives. Help each person feel less alone.

---

## Cross-Session Principles

All sessions should:
1. **Start with curiosity, not judgment**
2. **Help them observe themselves** as if they're watching a movie
3. **Point to their own wisdom** - don't impose external wisdom
4. **Create safety** to explore deep feelings
5. **End with integration** - a small practice or reflection to carry forward
6. **Leave them changed** - not just talked-to, but fundamentally shifted
